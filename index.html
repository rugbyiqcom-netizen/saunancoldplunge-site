Hot. Cold. One press each time you move.

Garmin Connect IQ Apple Watch: coming to the App Store

What it does

  • Press Start when you walk in. Press Next each time you move: Heat, Shower, Water, Breathe, Rest.
  • Times every round, counts your cycles, shows your heart rate in the heat and how fast it settles in the cold.
  • Saves the whole session as a workout in Apple Health or Garmin Connect.
  • Any order, any length. Sauna and a cold shower is a session. Sauna and the sea is a session.

Free. No account, no ads, no data leaves your watch. Not a medical device.
