Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

The short version: everything stays on your watch and phone. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We never see your data.

1. Who we are

Sauna ’n Cold Plunge (“the app”) is published by Gary Gold. It is a session timer for sauna and cold-water contrast sessions on Apple Watch (with an iPhone companion app) and on Garmin watches through Connect IQ. Questions about this policy go to saunancoldplunge@gmail.com.

2. What the app accesses on Apple Watch and iPhone

With your permission, the Apple Watch app uses Apple HealthKit to:

  • Read your heart rate during a session, so it can show your heart rate in the heat and how it settles in the cold.
  • Read active energy during a session, so the calorie figure on the summary is the one your watch calculates.
  • Save each session to Apple Health as a workout, with its duration, heart rate and active energy, so it appears in the Fitness and Health apps alongside your other activity.

On Apple Watch Ultra the app can use the water-detection sensor to confirm when you are in the water and to read the water temperature. On other models you type the water temperature yourself.

You can also enter your weight, height, age and sex in the app. These are used only to estimate calories and fluid loss and are stored on your devices.

3. What the app accesses on Garmin

The Garmin app reads heart rate, the watch’s temperature sensor and your Garmin user profile (weight, age) on the watch, and saves each session as an activity in Garmin Connect through Garmin’s own FIT recording. Garmin Connect is governed by Garmin’s privacy policy; we do not receive anything from it.

4. Where your data lives

Session history and your settings are stored locally on your watch and, for Apple, synced to your iPhone over Apple’s encrypted device-to-device WatchConnectivity link. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. We operate no servers and keep no copy of your data.

5. HealthKit commitments

  • Health data is used only to provide the features described above.
  • It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties.
  • It is never stored in iCloud by the app.

6. Tracking, analytics and advertising

None. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not track you across apps or websites.

7. Your control

  • Revoke Health access at any time: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Sauna ’n Cold Plunge.
  • Delete any saved workout in the Health or Fitness app.
  • Delete all app data by removing the app from your watch and phone.
  • On Garmin, delete the activity in Garmin Connect, or remove the app from the watch in the Connect IQ app.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 17, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Not medical advice

The app is a wellness timer, not a medical device. Heart rate, calorie and fluid-loss figures are estimates for your own information. Heat and cold put real stress on the heart and circulation. Talk to your doctor before contrast therapy if you have a heart condition, high or low blood pressure, are pregnant or have any other health concern.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
