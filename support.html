Support

Something confusing or wrong? Email saunaandcoldplunge@gmail.com with your watch model and, if you can, a screenshot. It is a small app made by one person, so you will get a real reply.

How a session works

  1. Press Start as you walk in and choose where you are going first (usually Heat).
  2. Each time you move, press the big Next button. It guesses your next phase; tap the name to pick a different one. Phases: Heat, Shower, Water, Breathe, Rest.
  3. Going into the water, type the water temperature off the tub thermometer. Your hand usually stays dry, so the watch cannot read it for you (Apple Watch Ultra reads it when submerged).
  4. When you are done: on Apple Watch swipe up and press End; on Garmin press BACK. Give the session a score out of five and save.

Any order and any length is a valid session. Sauna plus a cold shower counts. Sauna plus the sea counts.

Apple Watch

The watch app is not showing up

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Sauna ’n Cold Plunge. The app needs watchOS 11 or later.

No heart rate

Allow heart rate when the app asks, or turn it on in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Sauna ’n Cold Plunge. The watch needs to be snug on your wrist; in the ice bath the reading can drop out for a few seconds and come back.

Where did my session go?

It is saved as a workout in the Fitness and Health apps, and in the Sessions tab of the iPhone app (tap Sync on the Dashboard if it has not arrived yet).

Water Lock

Swipe up on the session screen and tap Lock before going in the water so wet-screen taps do not change your phase. Turn the crown to unlock.

Garmin

Installing

Search “Sauna n Cold Plunge” in the Connect IQ app on your phone. Works on Fenix 7 and 8, Epix, Enduro 2, Forerunner 265 and 965, Venu 2 and 3, Tactix 7 and 8 and Quatix 8.

Buttons

START moves to the next phase; hold START for the full list. UP and DOWN adjust the temperature. BACK ends the session. Hold UP on the ready screen for settings.

Heat temperature

The watch’s own sensor is used. It lags a few minutes behind the room, so the reading climbs through the first round; that is normal.

Safety

Heat and cold put real stress on the heart and circulation. Go gradually, never alone, and get out at the first sign of dizziness, numbness or chest discomfort. The app is a timer, not a medical device, and gives no medical advice.
